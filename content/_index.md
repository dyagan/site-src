Danny Ferris Yagan is the <a href="https://www.econ.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank">Robert D. Burch Professor of Economics</a> at the University of California, Berkeley; Co-founder and Chief Economist of the <a href="https://budgetlab.yale.edu" target="_blank">Budget Lab at Yale</a>; Director of the <a href="https://eml.berkeley.edu/~burch/" target="_blank">Berkeley Burch Center for Tax Policy and Public Finance</a>; Research Associate of the <a href="https://www.nber.org/" target="_blank">National Bureau of Economic Research</a>; and Co-editor and incoming Editor-in-Chief of the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-public-economics" target="_blank">Journal of Public Economics</a>.
